Car Rental Website

A clean, conversion-focused car rental site built to showcase vehicles clearly, make bookings easy to start, and earn trust before the first phone call.

Overview

What I built

This car rental website was built to solve a specific business problem: converting visitors into booking enquiries. The site showcases the vehicle fleet clearly, presents pricing and rental terms upfront, and provides multiple paths to contact — phone, WhatsApp and a booking form.

The design prioritizes trust and clarity. Vehicle listings include photos, specifications and pricing. The site structure guides visitors from browsing to enquiry with minimal friction. Every element serves a conversion purpose.

The Challenge

The problem I was solving

Car rental businesses in Nigeria often rely on social media posts and word-of-mouth. The challenge was building a website that establishes credibility, presents the fleet professionally, and makes it easy for potential customers to start a booking enquiry — whether through a form, phone call or WhatsApp message.

Key Features

What the site includes

01

Vehicle Listings

Clean vehicle showcase with photos, specifications, pricing and availability. Each vehicle has a dedicated section that presents all the information a customer needs.

02

Booking Enquiry

Multiple contact paths: phone, WhatsApp and a booking form. Visitors can start the booking process through whichever channel they prefer.

03

Trust Signals

The site structure and design are built to establish credibility — clean typography, professional photography and clear business information.

04

Responsive Design

The entire site works across desktop, tablet and mobile, with a layout that adapts to screen size and touch-friendly navigation.
