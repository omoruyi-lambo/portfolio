Everything you need to know about working with me as a freelance web developer in Nigeria.

Pricing depends on the project complexity: simple business websites start from ₦150,000, full‑stack restaurant/booking sites start from ₦350,000, and custom e‑commerce starts from ₦600,000. All prices include deployment, 3 months of free support, and training on how to update your site.

Most projects take 1–4 weeks. Simple business sites take 1–2 weeks, full‑stack sites with payments/orders take 3–4 weeks. I work fast and give clear timelines during our free consultation.

I accept bank transfers to Nigerian accounts, Paystack payments, and also cash on delivery for Benin City clients. I typically work with a 50% deposit upfront and 50% on delivery.
