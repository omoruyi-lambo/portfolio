Overview

What I built

Deskon Academy is a student portfolio platform designed to showcase tech talent. The platform lets students create profiles, display their projects and achievements, and makes it easy for recruiters and collaborators to discover and connect with emerging developers.

The site features a clean discovery interface where visitors can browse student profiles, filter by skills and view individual portfolios. Each student profile includes their projects, technical skills and contact information — presented in a professional, easy-to-scan format.

The Challenge

The problem I was solving

Early-career developers often struggle to get noticed. Traditional resumes don't show what someone can actually build, and GitHub profiles alone don't tell the full story. The challenge was creating a platform that presents student work in a professional, visual format — making it easier for recruiters and hiring managers to discover talent.

Key Features

What the application does

01

Student Profiles

Each student gets a dedicated profile page showcasing their skills, projects and achievements in a clean, professional format.

02

Project Showcase

Students can display their projects with descriptions, screenshots and links — giving visitors a clear view of what they've built.

03

Talent Discovery

Browse and discover students by browsing the platform. The interface is designed for quick scanning and easy navigation.

04

Responsive Design

The entire platform works across desktop, tablet and mobile, with layouts that adapt to screen size.
