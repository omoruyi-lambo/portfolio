How Much Does a Booking Website Cost in Nigeria in 2026?

Omoruyi Isaiah — Lambo Code Benin City, Nigeria
Published August 2026

Booking websites serve businesses that rely on appointments, reservations, or scheduled services. From hotels and car rentals to salons, event venues, and medical clinics — any business that manages time-based bookings needs a professional booking system. But what does it cost to build one in Nigeria?

What is a Booking Website?

A booking website allows customers to schedule appointments, make reservations, or book services online. Instead of calling or visiting in person, customers can check availability, select time slots, and confirm bookings through your website. This saves your staff time, reduces scheduling errors, and lets customers book 24/7.

Booking websites are used by:

  • Hotels and guest houses — Room reservations and availability management
  • Car rental companies — Vehicle booking and scheduling
  • Salons and barbershops — Appointment scheduling for services
  • Event venues — Space booking and availability calendars
  • Medical clinics — Patient appointment scheduling
  • Fitness centers — Class booking and personal training sessions
  • Service businesses — Any business that operates on appointments

What Affects Booking Website Cost?

The cost of a booking website depends on:

  • Complexity of booking logic — Simple appointment scheduling costs less than multi-resource booking with availability conflicts.
  • Number of bookable items — A single salon booking for one stylist is simpler than a hotel with dozens of rooms.
  • Payment integration — Booking with upfront payment requires Paystack or Flutterwave integration.
  • Calendar management — Real-time availability syncing, recurring bookings, and time zone handling add complexity.
  • User accounts — Customer login, booking history, and profile management increase development scope.
  • Notification systems — SMS and email reminders for bookings require additional integration.

Booking Website Pricing in Nigeria

Basic Appointment Booking System

₦350,000 – ₦500,000

Suitable for salons, barbershops, and small service businesses. Includes calendar display, time slot selection, customer information collection, booking confirmation, and basic admin dashboard for managing appointments. This is ideal for businesses that need to replace phone-based scheduling with a simple online system.

Standard Booking Platform with Payment

₦600,000 – ₦900,000

For hotels, car rentals, and event venues. Includes availability calendars, booking management, Paystack payment integration for deposits or full payments, customer accounts, booking history, email confirmations, and admin dashboard with revenue tracking. This handles more complex booking scenarios and payment processing.

Enterprise Booking Solution

₦1,200,000 – ₦2,500,000+

Custom booking system for complex operations. Multi-location support, resource allocation, advanced availability management, integration with property management systems, automated SMS/email reminders, waitlist functionality, dynamic pricing, and comprehensive analytics. This is for businesses with sophisticated booking needs.

Essential Features for Booking Websites

Regardless of your business type, a professional booking website should include:

  • Real-time availability display — Customers see what's actually available when they book
  • Easy booking flow — Simple steps to select date, time, and confirm booking
  • Confirmation system — Email or SMS confirmation sent automatically
  • Admin dashboard — Your team can view, manage, and modify bookings
  • Calendar integration — Sync with your existing calendars if needed
  • Payment processing — Collect deposits or full payments through Paystack
  • Reminder notifications — Reduce no-shows with automated reminders
  • Cancellation and rescheduling — Let customers manage their own bookings

Industry-Specific Considerations

Hotels and Guest Houses

Hotel booking systems need to handle room types, pricing variations by season, length of stay calculations, check-in/check-out times, and integration with property management systems. The booking flow should show room photos, amenities, and clear pricing breakdowns.

Car Rental Businesses

Car rental booking requires vehicle availability management, pricing by vehicle type and duration, insurance options, pickup/drop-off scheduling, and driver information collection. The system should handle vehicle categories, features, and clear rental terms.

Salons and Barbershops

Service-based booking needs to handle multiple staff members, service durations, break times, and recurring appointments. Customers should be able to select their preferred stylist or service provider and see their specific availability.

Event Venues

Event space booking requires calendar management for different venue areas, capacity limits, setup time requirements, deposit pricing, and event details collection. The system should handle inquiries, provisional bookings, and confirmations.

Payment Integration for Bookings

Many Nigerian businesses prefer to collect deposits or full payments at the time of booking. This reduces no-shows and secures revenue. Paystack integration is standard for Nigerian booking websites, allowing you to:

  • Collect deposits to secure bookings
  • Process full payments upfront
  • Offer refundable vs. non-refundable options
  • Handle partial payments and balances

Even if you don't require payment upfront, having the option gives you flexibility.

Mobile Optimization for Booking

Most customers will book from their phones. Your booking website must be mobile-optimized with:

  • Large, tappable date and time selectors
  • Clear booking flow that works on small screens
  • Fast loading even on slower connections
  • WhatsApp integration for booking inquiries
  • Simplified forms that don't frustrate mobile users

Managing Bookings on Your End

The admin dashboard is where your team manages bookings. It should provide:

  • Daily, weekly, and monthly calendar views
  • Quick booking status changes (confirmed, pending, cancelled)
  • Customer contact information and booking history
  • Revenue tracking and reporting
  • Ability to add bookings manually (for phone or walk-in customers)
  • Export functionality for accounting integration

Additional Costs to Consider

Beyond development, budget for:

  • SMS notifications — SMS gateways charge per message for booking reminders
  • Payment gateway fees — Paystack charges per transaction
  • Hosting — Booking sites need reliable hosting, budget ₦20,000 – ₦50,000 monthly
  • Maintenance — Regular updates, security patches, and feature additions

Getting Started with Your Booking Website

Before requesting a quote, prepare:

  • Your booking requirements — what exactly can customers book?
  • Your availability rules — hours, lead time, blackout dates
  • Your pricing structure — flat rates, time-based pricing, seasonal variations
  • Payment requirements — deposits, full payments, or pay-on-arrival
  • Integration needs — existing calendars, accounting systems, or property management software

Conclusion

A professional booking website transforms how your business handles appointments and reservations. It reduces administrative work, prevents double-bookings, lets customers book 24/7, and provides a better customer experience. For Nigerian businesses in hospitality, services, and appointments, a booking system is no longer a luxury — it's essential for competing in 2026.
