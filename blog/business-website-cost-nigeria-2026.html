How Much Does a Business Website Cost in Nigeria in 2026?

Omoruyi Isaiah — Lambo Code Benin City, Nigeria
Published August 2026

If you run a business in Nigeria and you're considering a professional website, one of the first questions is cost. Pricing varies widely depending on what you need, but understanding the tiers helps you budget correctly and avoid overpaying for features you don't use.

What Affects Business Website Cost in Nigeria?

Several factors determine what you'll pay for a business website. The main ones are:

  • Scope and functionality — A simple informational site costs less than one with booking systems, payment integration, or customer portals.
  • Design customization — Custom design built from scratch costs more than using templates or pre-built themes.
  • Backend requirements — Sites that need databases, admin dashboards, or API integrations require more development time.
  • Content and features — The number of pages, blog functionality, image galleries, and interactive elements all affect the scope.
  • Developer expertise — Experienced full-stack developers who deliver reliable, maintainable code charge more but save you money long-term.

Business Website Pricing Tiers in Nigeria

Basic Business Website (Informational)

₦120,000 – ₦180,000

A professional 5-8 page website with your business information, services, about section, contact form, and location details. This is ideal for small businesses, consultants, and service providers who need an online presence but don't require complex functionality. Built with clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for fast loading and easy maintenance.

Standard Business Website with CMS

₦250,000 – ₦400,000

Includes everything in the basic tier plus a content management system that lets you update text, images, and pages yourself. This is perfect for businesses that frequently update their services, add case studies, or publish blog content. The CMS is built to be intuitive — no technical knowledge required to keep your site current.

Advanced Business Website with Custom Features

₦450,000 – ₦800,000+

Full-stack business website with custom functionality such as booking systems, payment integration (Paystack), customer portals, admin dashboards, or inventory management. This tier is for businesses that need their website to do more than just display information — it needs to handle operations, automate processes, or integrate with existing business systems.

What's Included in a Professional Business Website?

When you work with a serious developer like Lambo Code, your business website should include:

  • Mobile-responsive design — Your site must work perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops. Nigerian customers browse primarily on mobile.
  • Fast loading speed — Optimized images, clean code, and proper hosting ensure your site loads quickly even on slower connections.
  • SEO basics — Proper meta tags, semantic HTML, and structure that helps search engines understand your business.
  • Contact functionality — Working contact forms, WhatsApp integration, or click-to-call buttons that make it easy for customers to reach you.
  • Professional branding — Consistent colors, fonts, and design that match your business identity.
  • Security basics — SSL certificates, secure forms, and protection against common vulnerabilities.

Additional Costs to Consider

Beyond the development cost, budget for:

  • Domain name — Typically ₦2,500 – ₦5,000 per year for a .com or .ng domain.
  • Web hosting — Shared hosting starts around ₦3,000 – ₦8,000 monthly, while better performance hosting costs ₦15,000 – ₦30,000 monthly.
  • Maintenance — Plan for periodic updates, security checks, and content updates. Some developers offer maintenance packages.
  • Content creation — Professional photography, copywriting, or video content if needed.

How to Choose the Right Tier for Your Business

Start with what your business actually needs. If you're a consultant or local service provider, a basic informational site might be all you need. If you run a business that takes bookings, processes payments, or needs to manage customer data, invest in the advanced tier from the start — building it right the first time costs less than upgrading later.

The key is to work with a developer who understands your business and can recommend the right scope rather than upselling features you won't use.

Why Invest in a Professional Business Website?

A professional website serves as your 24/7 salesperson. It builds credibility with customers who research businesses online before buying. It provides a central hub for your marketing, social media, and customer communications. And when built correctly, it becomes an asset that grows with your business.

In Nigeria's competitive market, businesses without professional websites lose customers to competitors who are easier to find and look more established online.
