How Much Does a Restaurant Website Cost in Nigeria? (2026 Guide)

Omoruyi Isaiah — Lambo Code Benin City, Nigeria
Published June 2026

If you own a restaurant, bar, or food business in Nigeria and you're wondering what a professional website costs — this guide is for you.

Why Your Restaurant Needs a Website in 2026

Customers search online before they eat out. If your restaurant doesn't have a professional website, you're losing customers to competitors who do. A well-built restaurant website builds trust, showcases your menu, and lets customers place orders directly — without going through third-party apps that take a cut of your revenue.

In Nigeria especially, customers are increasingly making decisions based on a restaurant's online presence before they ever step through the door. A premium website signals that you're serious, professional, and worth visiting.

What Affects the Cost of a Restaurant Website in Nigeria?

Basic Restaurant Website (Frontend Only)

₦150,000 – ₦200,000

A simple site with your menu, location, photos, and contact information. Good for visibility and building trust. Typically built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. This is the starting point for small restaurants that want to be findable online.

Full-Stack Restaurant Website with Paystack Integration

₦350,000 – ₦600,000

This is where serious restaurant businesses operate. Includes: online ordering, Paystack payment integration, real-time order management, push notifications for staff, and a full admin dashboard. This is what Lambo Code specialises in building — from scratch, tailored to your business.

Custom Features

Quoted Per Scope

Loyalty programs, reservation systems, delivery tracking, multi-branch dashboards, and WhatsApp ordering integration. Every feature adds value and increases the scope of the project. A good developer will help you decide which features actually matter for your specific business.

Don't pay for a website that just looks good. Pay for one that works hard for your business. A great restaurant website pays for itself in new customers within weeks of going live.
